[2026-10-18] The company owner may reopen a fiscal year closed with a bokslut in Accounted (founder decision, crm#1290), superseding the 2026-08-27 decision that only klarmarkera is undoable in the app and the 2026-10-18 closed-year guidance that sent this case to support. The reopen is lawful only while the årsredovisning has no legal weight, so it is refused (fail closed) on a submission, a signature, a signed/filed/registered version, a later closed year, a locked next year, a lock date over the year end, a Bolagsverket report for the year or later in the TIC snapshot, and whenever that snapshot is missing, older than 7 days, for another org number or without a complete report list (profile flag financialReportsComplete; "contact support"); the owner also restates the year's name and states the report was not signed, adopted at a stämma or filed outside Accounted, which no record here can show. Gate and state change are one SECURITY DEFINER RPC (migration 20261018120000, re-checked under a row lock), the stornos stay in the engine; the record is audit_log, not a new table: it is already immutable and feeds behandlingshistorik, and a new table with foreign keys would have needed sandbox teardown coverage. The year-end's currency revaluation is reversed too, since the re-run refuses while one is posted. Session route only, like the fiscal-year reset: an owner's statement about the årsredovisning is not given by an API key or an agent. Forms without an årsredovisning (enskild firma, ideell förening) are refused by the app and stay with support's scripts/undo-year-end-closing.ts, which remains the fallback when a gate refuses.
