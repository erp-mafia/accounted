[2026-10-21] The shared poll loop (src/components/bank-feed/poll-loop.ts) now sorts failures itself instead of each caller opting in: an HTTP 4xx other than 408/429 ends it at once and anything else is retried at most 10 times in a row, with onGiveUp telling the caller why. Chosen over adding an onError to the one Koppla banken call (the triage's fix) because none of the four callers had wired onError, so the forever-retry was the default everywhere; a refused consent poll now shows Börja om, a silent one Försök igen. The unattended Open Payments sync's handling of a permanent consent refusal (status 'error' with "Försök igen om en stund", retried every 6 h) is the same class but left for a founder decision, since it changes connection states and notices (crm#1355).
