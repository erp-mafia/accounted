[2026-10-19] A bank payment's debtor BIC is the paying account's connection BIC first (bank_connections.bic, the bank the Open Payments consent read the account from and the X-BicFi every payment call sends), then the IBAN clearing table, then the bank-name table (crm#1314): the tables named only the big banks, so an Open Payments account at any other bank (Lunar) was refused as bank_unknown in the account list and in every prepare. The connection wins over a table that disagrees, with no conflict refusal: the payment goes to the provider, whose identifier the connection holds, and no account in production disagrees today. Enable Banking connections store no BIC and keep the tables, so their accounts outside them stay refused until they move to the new connection. Over MCP, PAYMENT_ORDERS_ACCOUNT_NOT_PAYABLE now carries details.reason as on v1; payments.availability was not bound as a new MCP tool, since a dry run of gnubok_prepare_payments answers the same question per account.
