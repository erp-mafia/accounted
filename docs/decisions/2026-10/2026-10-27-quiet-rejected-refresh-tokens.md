[2026-10-27] Rejected refresh tokens (refresh_token_not_found, refresh_token_already_used) are logged by auth-js itself with console.error from the INITIAL_SESSION event that @supabase/ssr's createServerClient subscription triggers, with no option to switch it off, so the proxy narrows exactly that one console call (single argument, an auth-js error with one of the two codes) into an info/warn line without stack instead of patching the library or wrapping every console.error; cross-request refresh races are left as they are because single-flighting a refresh across requests needs a shared lock store and the observed already-used case was a rotated cookie that never reached the browser (O15).
