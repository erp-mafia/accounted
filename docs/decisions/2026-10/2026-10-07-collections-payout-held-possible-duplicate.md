[2026-10-07] A collections payout held as a possible duplicate (a bank row of its amount and reference already on a voucher booked by hand) is released only by a person: "not a duplicate" records who and when and books it, and the guard does not ask again for that payout; "handled elsewhere" marks it ignored with the person's reason and it is never booked. Both decisions are written to audit_log by a trigger on collection_settlements with the person, so no write path can make one without a trace; ignoring is offered only for a possible duplicate, never as a general way to drop a payout.
