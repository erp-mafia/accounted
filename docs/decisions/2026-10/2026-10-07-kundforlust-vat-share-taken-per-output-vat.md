[2026-10-07] The kundförlust VAT share is taken per output VAT account from the invoice's own posted voucher (net of its credit notes' vouchers), and the ruta 05 base per share is vat * 100 / rate to the öre rather than read from the revenue lines: revenue accounts carry no rate on the line, and ruta 05 is declared in whole kronor, so the öre rounding never reaches the declaration.
