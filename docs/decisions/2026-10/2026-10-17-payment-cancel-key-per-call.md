[2026-10-17] Two Connect payments idempotency keys revised from 2026-10-10. A cancel is keyed per call (`cancel:<payment id>:<uuid>`), not per payment: Connect keeps a key's first answer for 30 days, replaying it to the same body and refusing another body as CONNECTOR_IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH without asking the bank, and the body carries the party (IP, user agent, signer), which differs between the browser, an agent and the status sync, so one unconfirmed cancel locked the payment against every later cancel, the recreate after an expired signing included (crm#1282). A cancel answer (too late, needs signing) holds only for its moment and asking a bank to cancel twice cannot pay anything, so cancel joins the signing start as a write without replay. A basket is keyed by its signing and payments (`basket:<payment_order_batches.id>:<sha256 of the sorted payment ids>`), not by the payments alone: a restart of a failed signing whose payments the bank kept is a new signing over the same payments, which got the dead basket replayed or a mismatch; a retry inside one signing still names the same basket. The CONNECTOR_IDEMPOTENCY_MISMATCH mapping (a final refusal) stays: with these keys and the attempt-numbered create keys no known path reuses a key with another body.
