[2026-10-23] A kreditfaktura of a kontantmetod ROT/RUT invoice reverses what the ledger holds, not the whole share (#93): the customer's share plus what the payout booked of Skatteverket's share by the payout's own per-invoice rule (all when paid in full, the beslut's amount when partly), with 1513 credited that much as owed back; with no payout, or a payout in a later räkenskapsår than the payment, the share is reversed whole because the year end carries it onto 1513; a payout after the credit stays on 1513 (owed back, not a sale); a partial beslut over several invoices without per-invoice amounts is refused, never guessed (ML 2023:200 7 kap. 16 §; Skatteverket, Så fungerar rotavdraget).
