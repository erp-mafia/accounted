[2026-10-22] Registering an issued, unpaid invoice under kontantmetoden (original number and date, no verifikat until payment) is not built as an extension of register_booked_invoice: that door derives the whole header from a linked 151x verifikat, so a kontant variant is a new door (caller-supplied subtotal, VAT and treatment, header-only row, journal_entry_id NULL, counter untouched) and waits for the founder (PH 187, crm#1303).
