[2026-10-09] The collections clearing account cannot be changed while collected payments are booked on it and not yet paid out by a booked payout (field error clearing_account_in_use): a payout credits the account the connection names when it is booked, so a change in between would leave the old account debited and the new one short. Refusing the change was chosen over crediting each case's own C voucher account, which would let one payout voucher clear two accounts with no setting saying why.
