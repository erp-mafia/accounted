[2026-10-18] A migration customer stub built from an invoice (no private/company type from the source) whose identity number has personnummer shape (calendar month in digits 3-4, so never a legal person's organisationsnummer; no Luhn requirement, so a mistyped personnummer is protected too) becomes customer_type individual with the number encrypted in personal_number and org_number empty, unless the party carries a VAT number or a non-Swedish country. Chosen over keeping swedish_business (the enskild firma reading): the two cannot be told apart from the invoice, the file import already defaults an untyped personnummer to individual (lib/import/shared/classify.ts), a wrong individual costs one type change on the card while a wrong business keeps a personnummer in plaintext at rest (and in parties.org_number via link_party_on_role_write) and treats a consumer as a business in collections; ROT/RUT (HUSFL, privatperson only) can then read the card's personnummer. Register records the source typed (company or private) are unchanged, so an enskild firma typed company keeps its org number. Applies to the durable job worker and the legacy /migrate step. Existing stub rows are not touched by the code: an encrypting repair needs the Vercel-only key and is a founder-run script (crm#1294 follow-up).
