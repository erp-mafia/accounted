[2026-10-20] A Kivra payslip sends the structured salaryslip object next to its PDF, because Kivra's Tenant API requires it for type salaryslip (letter.salary is deprecated): the object names the employer with address, email and phone, so a company without them in its settings is refused before anything is sent (DIGITAL_MAILBOX_EMPLOYER_CONTACT_MISSING) rather than every payslip failing at Kivra; Billo shows the PDF only and is not affected. The phone goes in national form (0812345678) since Kivra's telephone pattern accepts a number that starts with a digit however its `+` branch is read.
