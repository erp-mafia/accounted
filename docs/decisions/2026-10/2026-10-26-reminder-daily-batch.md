[2026-10-26] The daily reminder batch (reminder_daily_batch) is its own staged operation type modelled on the collections batch, but reuses that batch's removals table and function (collection_batch_removals gains kind 'reminder'; remove_collection_batch_items accepts both types) instead of a parallel table, and the expiry and owner helpers moved to lib/pending-operations/daily-batch.ts for both. Items whose bank was not synced in 24 hours (or has no bank) are staged marked, not excluded: many small companies have no bank feed, and the approver sending knowingly is the spec's "a person approves" (never automatic). No typed confirmation word: a reminder is lighter than an inkasso step, and the per-row review plus a "Skicka N påminnelser" button is the deliberate act. Gated on the email_send capability like an invoice send.
