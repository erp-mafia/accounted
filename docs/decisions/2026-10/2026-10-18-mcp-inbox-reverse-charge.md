[2026-10-18] The MCP inbox supplier invoice (gnubok_create_supplier_invoice_from_inbox and its executor) resolves its VAT treatment through resolveSupplierInvoiceReverseCharge like the dashboard and v1 (accounted#58): no override means omvänd skattskyldighet for an EU or non-EU supplier; an override is the caller's explicit word (as reverse_charge is on v1), so 'exempt' on a foreign supplier is kept but named to the approver in compliance_warning, because 42 of the 43 such invoices in prod were explicit 'exempt' overrides, and exempt is right for some foreign purchases (financial services, insurance, passenger transport). Only the treatment is staged, not a reverse_charge flag: the grant door's supplier_invoice_operation_unfit refuses unknown params. The executor treats the staged treatment as explicit (what the approver saw) and applies the supplier default only when none is staged, so an op staged before the fix with the old standard_25 default commits as approved (0 such ops pending in prod). The shared resolver now also reads a stated treatment of reverse_charge without the flag as reverse charge (it resolved to the flag off; 0 such rows in prod). Existing invoices are not repaired in code: the customer or byrå corrects them with storno or rättelse.
