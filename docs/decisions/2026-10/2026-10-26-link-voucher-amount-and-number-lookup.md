[2026-10-26] Koppla till verifikat proposes min(invoice remainder, what the verifikat has left) as an editable amount, offers vouchers other invoices already use part of, and finds a verifikat named by its number at any date (?voucher=), instead of widening the due-date window: a kundförlust verifikat is often booked a year after the due dates, a wider window would bury every invoice in unrelated vouchers, and the link still sets the invoice paid (writing off against an existing verifikat stays a founder decision) (crm#1434).
