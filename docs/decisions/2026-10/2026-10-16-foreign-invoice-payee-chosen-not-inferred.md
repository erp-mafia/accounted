[2026-10-16] A foreign-currency invoice with no account chosen for its currency offers the accounts that can carry it (one click, any account that prints an IBAN or whose bank connection knows one, a SEK account included) instead of inferring one at render time (crm#1273): resolveInvoicePaymentAccount keeps refusing to fall back from EUR to the SEK default, because the printed payee is the company's word to its customer and a silent IBAN fallback would also change re-rendered PDFs and Peppol payment means for every company without a EUR choice. The inline first-details form is no longer offered on a foreign invoice: it saves SEK details only and then let the send through to a server refusal. No migration, no resolver or send-guard change.
