[2026-10-21] An opening balance (balance-sheet only) feeds the momsdeklaration only when it is dated after the declared period's first day and the company has no other posted verifikat of the period before it; otherwise it and its storno/rättelse chain stay out, and a counted chain is kept out of the momsredovisning shapes so a pair nets (crm#1333, migration 20261021000500): for a company that keeps books, VAT is reported for the period in which it is booked or should have been booked (ML 2023:200 7 kap. 14 § output, 31 § input; SFL 26 kap. 5 § 2 st., 10-11 §§), so a first-day IB re-declared earlier periods, and an IB inside the period is the right carrier only when the period's earlier days are not in the books (a mid-period migration); with them in the books (year-end roll-forward into a räkenskapsår starting mid-period, an import that brought them) it counted them twice. Neither "always count" (20260713120000) nor "never count" nor "date alone" (earlier versions of this MR) is right. An entry tagged opening_balance that books a resultatkonto is an ordinary verifikat, since no IB door writes class 3-8. The 2650/1650 shape is untouched. The SQL returns counted_opening_balances and carriedIntoPeriod (crm#1316) reads it, so the matcher and the declaration cannot disagree.
