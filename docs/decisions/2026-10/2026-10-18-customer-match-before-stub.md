[2026-10-18] The durable migration worker matches each unmapped source customer against the company's own customer cards before the commit, and records a match as the source mapping (adopt_provider_migration_invoice_party), instead of letting resolve_provider_migration_party create a stub (crm#1294). Order: org number or personnummer (decrypted app-side, which is why this is not SQL), VAT number, the provider's customer number typed into customer_number (only when the names share a word of 3+ letters, so a user's own numbering cannot pass for a match), then a normalised name (case, whitespace). A name match needs no conflicting org number, personnummer, VAT or customer number on the card, never takes a card another source customer of the connection already maps to, and two equal candidates mean no match. Strong keys may share a claimed card (one person entered twice at the source). Only non-archived cards created before the job are candidates, so the job never folds its own new cards (two departments under one org number keep two cards). Existing mappings are authoritative: a re-run re-matches nothing. No migration. Suppliers and the legacy /migrate orchestrator path are unchanged (follow-up).
