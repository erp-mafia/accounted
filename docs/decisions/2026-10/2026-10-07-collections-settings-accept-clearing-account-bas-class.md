[2026-10-07] The collections settings accept a clearing account in BAS class 1 outside 19xx (a receivable on the provider, default 1689) and a payout account in 19xx (cash and bank, where the payout's bank row lands), different from each other and resolvable in the chart: a cash account as clearing account would mix the provider's money into the bank balance, and an account the engine cannot resolve would refuse every booking later. The provider is never told about these; the database audits each change with the person.
