[2026-10-22] An avgiftsunderlag override of 0 kr on a payslip that is not an F-skatt payee's is the reviewer's statement that the pay is no underlag for socialavgifter, and the AGI now declares it in FK131/FK132-FK134/FK137-FK138 instead of FK011/FK012-FK018/FK041-FK043 (crm#1372); the save and the generator refuse it unless the avgift is 0 too, so the booking, FK487 and the IUs agree. The engine does not infer the 1 000-kronorsregel (SAL 2 kap. 14 §) from year-to-date pay: the test is the year's final total ("under året understigit"), and in production 3 of the 4 recipient-years under 1 000 kr in 2026 are hourly staff on ordinary payroll (one already passes 1 000 kr in its next draft run), so an automatic exemption would mostly exempt the wrong months. Teknisk beskrivning 1.1.18.2 avsnitt 5.4 allows an automatic version (declare as avgiftsfri, then in the month the year reaches 1 000 kr declare the year's pay as underlag and the earlier part in FK010 AvrakningAvgiftsfriErs); that moves avgifter between months users see, so it is left to the founders. A partial basis override keeps the filed underlag: no IU field can carry it.
