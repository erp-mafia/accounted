[2026-10-29] The extension dispatcher refuses viewers on every company-context write method (POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE) unless the route declares viewerMay, instead of gating the ungated routes one by one: a new route is closed by default. viewerMay is set only where the handler writes nothing (suggest-booking, VAT validate, skattekonto group-match candidates) or decides the viewer case itself (enable-banking sync inserts raw rows only for a viewer; mail and WhatsApp let a person revoke their own grant), which matches the door catalog in src/lib/permissions/doors.ts.
