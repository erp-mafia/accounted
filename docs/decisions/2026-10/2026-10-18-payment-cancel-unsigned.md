[2026-10-18] A bank cancel refused as too late (405) for a payment the bank reports unsigned (RCVD or PDNG, the statuses Connect maps to submitted) answers PAYMENT_CANCEL_UNSIGNED_REFUSED ("not signed, not paid without a signature, try again in a few minutes") only when no signing of the order finished in Accounted (submitted, or awaiting_signature in a signing still open); a finished signing the bank still answers RCVD for keeps PAYMENT_CANCEL_TOO_LATE, as does a refusal without a status (crm#1282). The unsigned flag is computed in the Connect adapter, not by a second status read, and is optional on the port so a provider that does not say stays too late. ACTC is not treated as unsigned: Connect maps it to accepted. Signera igen on a Betalningar row is offered only for a submitted order with a payment at the bank whose signing is back to created (abandoned or expired BankID, or never started), not for a signing stopped as failed, whose payments the bank was asked to cancel; it reuses restartPaymentSigning through the existing pendingBatchId flow. No migration.
