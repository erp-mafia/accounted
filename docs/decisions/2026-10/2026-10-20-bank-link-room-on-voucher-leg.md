[2026-10-20] A bank row links to a posted verifikat only if it fits in what other rows leave of its bank leg, counted per direction (not net, so a verifikat moving the account both ways takes its rows in any order); else VOUCHER_BANK_LEG_CONSUMED (GitHub #3507). The first row in a direction still links whatever its size (prod 2026-09-20..10-20: of 23 links the rule would have caught, 18 were second rows on an already-held leg and 5 were a lone row off by an öresavrundning or a few kronor, which keep linking and show on the status card).
