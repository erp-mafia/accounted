[2026-10-22] Webshop syncs read the Swedish day, and a booked row stored on the UTC day before the change is not flagged "changed in the store" (#58): ingest's freeze check treats a stored order/paid date exactly one day before the incoming one as the UTC day of the same instant, because nothing stores the source timestamp to tell the two apart and a false badge would claim the store changed a booked order; the cost is that a real store-side edit moving a booked order's date exactly one day later is not flagged (the verifikat keeps its date either way). Unbooked rows move onto the Swedish day in place on their next re-poll (keyed on external_id, no duplicates); booked rows and their verifikat are not rewritten.
