Taken without a real LF sample (none exists in prod or the ticket), so the parser is tolerant by design. Two judgement calls, taken as recommended: (1) description is the Text/Meddelande column, falling back to the Typ column, where the old positional parse gave every row of the bank's "Transaktioner" CSV its type ("Kortköp"; 909 rows in 15 companies in prod); (2) a header-less file's booking date is whichever leading date column is later, not always the second. Cost: a re-import overlapping an LF CSV period imported before this change may not dedup rows whose description or date changed (visible, removable with undo); Handelsbanken and Nordea Företag detection step aside for a header with a booking date beside a transaction date, which neither bank's export has.
