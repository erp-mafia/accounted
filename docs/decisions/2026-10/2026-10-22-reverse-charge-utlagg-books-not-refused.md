[2026-10-22] A supplier invoice paid privately under reverse charge (crm#1377) is booked, not refused: the utlägg line builder books the same reverse-charge sets as the registration verifikat (groupReverseChargeByRate, generateReverseChargePurchaseLines) and the person is owed the net, since who paid does not change who reports the moms (ML 2023:200 6 kap. 33 §, 16 kap. 6 §). Chosen over handing registerExpenseClaim its reverse_charge field, which books one cost account at 25 % for services only, collapsing multi-row invoices and refusing EU goods, 12/6 % and a 4535 row on a non-EU supplier that the AP registration accepts. The fiktiv moms is computed in the invoice currency and converted at the claim rate with its base, as the existing custom-lines utlägg path already does; on a foreign-currency invoice it can sit a few öre from 25 % of the SEK base, inside the declaration check tolerance (max 1 kr, 0.5 %).
