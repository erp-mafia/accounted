[2026-10-21] Invoice line VAT has one rule, computeLineVat = roundOre(net x rate / 100) in lib/bookkeeping/invoice-line-amounts.ts, used by the builder, recurring invoices, ROT/RUT, previews, the editor and the Peppol check, instead of teaching the check a one-öre tolerance: a tolerance would leave stored VAT wrong on exact halves while every surface still disagreed. Tie-break is roundOre's (half away from zero on positives); Peppol BIS 3.0 section 9 only fixes two decimals. Already issued invoices keep their stored VAT (crm#1343).
