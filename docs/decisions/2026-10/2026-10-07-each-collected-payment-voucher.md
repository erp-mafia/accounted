[2026-10-07] Each collected-payment voucher (step C) gets a payment record the ledger renders from the provider's payment data (invoice, case, amounts, payment day, the provider's payment reference, when and by whom it was booked), archived WORM before the voucher and linked to it as underlag; a booking refused after the archive keeps that one record on the row and uses it on the next attempt, so retries never leave unlinked copies. The settlement PDF reaches each C voucher it settles as its own archived copy (a document links to one voucher), with a deterministic id per settlement and voucher so a rerun after a crash finds it; the payout is marked booked only after the copies exist.
