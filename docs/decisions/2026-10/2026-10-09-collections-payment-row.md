[2026-10-09] A collections payment row (invoice_payments.source 'collections') may be deleted when its voucher is cancelled, and settleInvoicePayment now cancels the orphan voucher before it removes the row: before, a settlement that lost its CAS race cancelled the voucher but the delete guard kept the row, and the next booking attempt marked the collected payment booked on a cancelled voucher with the invoice still open. Only cancel_orphaned_entry cancels a posted voucher (a fresh orphan), so such a row carries no booking; the collected-payment booking also removes a row it finds in that state and never marks a payment booked on a voucher that is not posted.
