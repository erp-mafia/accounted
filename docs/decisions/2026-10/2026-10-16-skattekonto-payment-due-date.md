[2026-10-16] Moms and AGI due the same day are paid as one skattekonto payment through a combined source (source due_date: everything declared due on that day), not by relaxing the one-open-order index, so no migration (founder option a, crm#1260). Each part is read by its own resolver, so amount and due-date rules live in one place; a part that asks for nothing (refund in ruta 49, zero, already paid, period not ended) is left out and listed, never netted, because a refund reaches the skattekonto only when Skatteverket processes the declaration. Paying one declaration alone keeps replacing an unapproved draft (v1 rule) but warns same_due_date_parts, naming the other parts and whether a draft is replaced; the warning is advisory and is dropped, never the payment, when the other parts cannot be read.
