[2026-10-07] The collections sync run keeps a fixed reserve (45 s of the 270 s budget) for the settlement work: the case work, the sweep and the reconcile before it stop that much early, and the collected-payment and payout work then start nothing once the run's time is spent (counted as deferred). Without the reserve a large nightly reconcile could take the whole budget every night and the payout import, which runs only on the nightly run, would never get a turn.
