[2026-11-15] Dröjsmålsränta to a private person is computed at no more than referensräntan plus 20 percentage points, whatever rate the company set: konsumentkreditlagen (2026:1011) 4 kap. 4 §, same ceiling as 19 a § of the 2010 act, so no date gate. The setting itself still accepts a higher rate, because it is lawful between companies; the ceiling is applied where interest is computed and the report says when it lowered a rate.
