[2026-11-10] A skattekonto payment waiting for its BankID counts as cover only for UNANSWERED_SIGNING_MINUTES (60) after its signing started, unless the BankID finished; nothing is cancelled, Hem and the Skattekonto page ask for the money again: that is where the status sync itself stops waiting for a BankID, so a transient read failure no longer hides a real shortfall for days (crm#1721), and a payment signed but unconfirmed past the hour risks a refundable overpayment rather than kostnadsränta on a charge nobody pays.
