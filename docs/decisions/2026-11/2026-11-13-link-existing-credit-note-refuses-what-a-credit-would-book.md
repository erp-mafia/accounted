[2026-11-13] invoices.link-credit-note (crm#1852) links an existing credit note to its original as the kundreskontra side of an ordinary credit only (credit note gets credited_invoice_id and rests at 'sent', original moves to 'credited' through markOriginalInvoiceCredited) and never books: it refuses every pair creditNoteNeedsJournalEntry says a credit would have booked (faktureringsmetoden always, kontantmetoden once the original was paid or booked), ROT/RUT, an original with a kundförlust decision, a partial credit and an original that already has a credit note: inventing the missing verifikat, or leaving 'credited' on an invoice the customer still owes part of, would be worse than a refusal that names the reason. The imported credit note moves from 'credited' to 'sent' because a credit note resting at 'credited' is left out of the kundreskontra's netting (isSettledCreditNote) on the premise that the source system already netted it into the original, which is exactly what did not happen for these pairs. The migration import does not infer the link from free text: that is a guess on customer data and stays a founder decision.
