[2026-11-13] Bank row lines that do not balance are refused where they are built (buildTransactionEntryLines throws TransactionLinesNotBalancedError, a 400 TX_LINES_NOT_BALANCED with both totals), not repaired: only a learned line pattern can produce them (lines netting on the bank's own side, a moms line opposite its cost lines, a tax-only pattern with a remainder), and booking such a row mirrored or re-sided would change what the books say on a guess. The check is the engine's own lineBalance, so nothing that booked before is refused. Left open for the founders: whether a two-sided or tax-heavy pattern should mirror on the opposite sign, and whether the 3740 "öresutjämning" remainder of a pattern should be capped at a few öre (today it takes any remainder the ratios leave).
