[2026-11-13] A supplier invoice's "attested" and "overdue" are read facts with one home (lib/supplier-invoices/reported-status.ts), not the stored status: attested is approved_at or the stored status approved (a payment undo and an undone credit rest an invoice there with no mark), overdue is money left after the due date in a payable status, a part-paid invoice included. The Attesterade tab and status=approved / status=overdue in the dashboard list, the v1 API and the agent's list read them; a returned row keeps its stored status, so status=approved can return a row that reads overdue and status=overdue one that reads partially_paid, as the customer invoice filters already do (lib/invoices/overdue.ts). Read side only: the nightly flip, the stored column and every other status filter are unchanged, and a disputed invoice is not reported as overdue. The row chip still shows the stored status (a part-paid invoice past due reads Delbetald, as on the customer side).
