[2026-11-12] Calendar invites, S/MIME and PGP signatures, contact cards and delivery reports in an inbound mail are ignored (history only, no inbox row) when the mail also carries a part that can be a document, by a closed list of content types: their rejection rows read as "could not be read" beside the invoice that was read (crm#1809). Not widened to every unsupported type or to small images: a TIFF, ZIP or XML may be the receipt in a format to send again, so those keep their row.
