[2026-11-04] crm#1654: OAuth redirect URIs are unique per user, not platform-wide. A tool with one fixed callback for all its customers (squidler.io, vscode.dev) worked only for the first customer who registered it; every other customer got "already registered" and could not use the first one's row either. /authorize, /token and /register now resolve the consenting user's own registration first, then a colleague's; the trust boundary (exact match, own or shared-company registrations only) is unchanged. Chose this over adding squidler as a built-in client: it fixes every shared-callback tool, not one.
