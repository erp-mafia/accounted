[2026-11-13] The supplier invoice header change (due date, reference, number, dates, notes) is one service, lib/supplier-invoices/update-header.ts, behind the dashboard PUT, the v1 PATCH and gnubok_update_supplier_invoice (crm#1848). The operation supplier-invoices.update has an MCP binding only: the hand-written v1 PATCH stays its v1 door because its public contract is narrower (registered only, a register-only key, status label left to the daily check, SI_NOT_DRAFT) and passes those as a door gate that can refuse more, never less. Staged at medium risk like update_invoice: nothing is booked, but the due date and reference steer a payment. auto_debit is left out of the tool: v1 never took it either.
