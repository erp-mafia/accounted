[2026-11-12] The bokslut's schablonintäkt base reads every periodiseringsfond account of INK2R 2.29 (211x-213x, taken from the INK2R mapping), not only 212x: IL 30 kap. 6 a § counts every fond held at the start of the year. A 213x fond gets the year of its 212x twin for the six-year limit; a 211x fond names no year, so it is never forced back and the proposal warns instead. Which account a new avsättning is booked on (212x by year) is unchanged, and so are rounding and proration (crm#1797).
