[2026-11-12] INK2S 4.6a (schablonintäkt on periodiseringsfonder, SRU 7654) follows IL 30 kap. 6 a § for every räkenskapsår of whole months, brutet, short and long included: the opening balance of the INK2R 2.29 accounts (2110-2139) times statslåneräntan at the end of November of the year before the calendar year in which the beskattningsår ENDS (the start date never picks the rate), at least 0.5 %, with no 72 % factor (that was the wording before SFS 2018:1206), times whole months / 12, in whole kronor with the öre dropped. Sourced: the rate year, the floor, the basis and that a year longer or shorter than twelve months is adjusted "i motsvarande mån" (law text; Skatteverket SKV 294 utgåva 24 punkt 4.6 a; rates from Riksgälden). Inference, not stated by any source: counting whole calendar months over 12 (supported by the law's "tolv månader" and Skatteverket's month-based depreciation example), and truncating instead of rounding (no source decides; at most 1 krona; chosen because the rest of the form drops öre). Because the months rule is an inference, a year it adjusts also gets a warning asking the user to check the amount. Fail closed, 0 plus a warning naming the opening fonder: a year that starts or ends mid-month or is not 1 to 18 months (no source on part months), a year started before 2019, a closing year whose rate is not in the table yet, a fond account opening in debit. The founder decided to follow the sourced rule instead of calendar years only (crm#1797). Not changed here: the bokslut tax provision and INK3 still count only 212x, round instead of truncate and do not adjust for the length of the year.
