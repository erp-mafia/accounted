This page explains how you may use them.

In short: use the code freely, use the name honestly, and never make people think something comes from us when it does not. Renaming a fork changes nothing about your AGPL duties: if you modify Accounted and let others use it over a network, you still offer them your source.

## Fine without asking

- **Running Accounted for yourself.** Running Accounted, modified or not, for your own company or organisation, on your own domain or a subdomain such as `accounted.yourcompany.se`.
- **Accounting firms.** An accounting firm or consultant may run an unmodified Accounted for its own clients under the Accounted name, as long as it says clearly that the firm, not Arcim Technology AB, runs the service.
- **Passing on unmodified releases.** Redistributing an unmodified release (for example a Docker image built from an unchanged release tag) under the Accounted name.
- **Stating facts.** Writing "built on Accounted", "compatible with Accounted" or "imports from Accounted" in plain text, without our logo, as long as nothing suggests that we endorse or support your product.
- **Naming add-ons.** Naming an extension, connector, integration or MCP server "*Your name* for Accounted", never "Accounted *your name*".
- **Talking about Accounted.** Articles, tutorials, reviews, talks and comparisons, including showing the logo to refer to Accounted itself.

## Requires your own name

If you offer a modified version of Accounted to others (as a hosted service, a distribution, a resale or a white-label product):

- Give it your own name and logo. [docs/WHITELABEL.md](docs/WHITELABEL.md) shows how: one branding extension or a few environment variables replace every user-visible brand reference.
- Do not use "Accounted", "gnubok" or a confusingly similar name in your product, company, domain or package names (for example "Accounted Pro", "AccountedHQ", "Acounted").
- You may say "based on Accounted" once, in plain text, together with: "Not affiliated with or endorsed by Arcim Technology AB."

## Requires our written permission

- Offering Accounted to others as a hosted service under the Accounted name or logo, even unmodified.
- Domain names, company names, social accounts or package names that contain "accounted" or "gnubok".
- Using the logo in ads, on merchandise or in a way that suggests a partnership.
- Calling anything "official" or "certified", or using names we reserve for our own services: Accounted Connect, Accounted MCP, the `accounted-*` and `gnubok-*` npm package names, the `@accounted` npm scope, and `accounted.*` / `gnubok.*` domains.

## The former name gnubok

Accounted was called gnubok until 2026. The old name still appears in machine-facing identifiers (`gnubok-mcp`, `app.gnubok.se`, API key prefixes) and is kept there on purpose. The same rules apply to it.
