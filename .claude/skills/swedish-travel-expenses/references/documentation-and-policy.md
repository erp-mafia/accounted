## 1. Reseräkning

Skatteverket's minimum for paying tax-free traktamente and milersättning:

- name, personnummer and home address;
- the tjänsteställe with address;
- start and end of the trip, dates and times;
- purpose of the trip;
- kilometres driven, for milersättning;
- free meals received.

Add as good practice: destination and itinerary per day, overnight location each night, and for trips abroad the countries visited with border-crossing times.

## 2. Körjournal

Skatteverket accepts the employee's körjournal as underlag for bilersättning. Per trip: date, route (from and to), business purpose, distance in mil or km, and preferably odometer readings. The distance must also appear on the reseräkning.

## 3. Verifikationer and egen verifikation

- A verifikation shows when it was compiled, when the transaction happened, what it concerns, the amount, the counterparty, and a verifikationsnummer (BFL 5 kap. 7 §).
- Received documents must be used as the verifikation (BFL 5 kap. 6 §). Additions must show when, by whom and what was added (BFNAR 2013:2 punkt 5.8).
- **Lost receipt:** the company may compile an egen verifikation with all BFL details (BFNAR 2013:2 punkt 5.2). It gives **no VAT deduction** and weakens the income-tax deduction if challenged. Require a signed statement from the employee, proof of payment (card or bank statement), purpose and, for meals, attendees. Applying punkt 5.2 to lost employee receipts is our reading; BFN does not mention the case specifically.

## 4. Language

Information the company drafts itself (reseräkningar, egna verifikationer) must be in Swedish, Danish, Norwegian or English (BFL 1 kap. 4 §). BFN states there is no language requirement for received documents, so foreign-language receipts need no translation. Noting what, amount and VAT in the reseräkning is still good practice.

## 5. Arkivering and scanning

- Keep all räkenskapsinformation for **7 years** after the end of the calendar year in which the financial year ended (BFL 7 kap. 2 §).
- Since **2024-07-01** (SFS 2024:342), paper receipts may be destroyed immediately after scanning, provided the transfer carries no risk of the information being changed or lost. Document the scanning routine.
- BFNAR 2013:2 was amended by BFNAR 2024:1. Fading thermal receipts must be transferred to a durable medium (punkt 7.7).
- Electronic originals (e-mailed receipts, PDF invoices) are kept electronically.

## 6. Special cases

| Case | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Blandad resa (private days added) | Only the business part is deductible or tax-free. If the trip is mainly private, the travel itself is a benefit. Document the programme per day |
| Family member travels along | Their costs are a taxable benefit or a non-deductible private cost, unless they have a documented business role |
| Conference abroad | Needs a substantial business programme to avoid benefit taxation. Conference-package meals reduce traktamente |
| Bonuspoäng and upgrades used privately | Taxable benefit, valued at what the employee would otherwise have paid. Employee must tell the employer no later than the month after use. Points used for new business trips: no benefit |
| Cancellation and rebooking fees | Deductible if the trip was for business. Deduct VAT only if shown on the invoice |
| Tjänstereseförsäkring | Normally a business cost. Cover that extends to private travel may be a benefit (not verified for 2026) |
| Board member with styrelsearvode as employment income | May receive tax-free traktamente and milersättning under the same rules |
| Consultant with F-skatt | Travel is invoiced as part of the fee, normally with VAT; no traktamente rules apply |
| CO2 compensation for business travel | Operating cost (for example **5890** or **6991**) with VAT as invoiced. No authoritative 2026 guidance found |

## 7. Common audit errors

Errors that make Skatteverket reclassify payments as salary or reverse deductions:

1. Traktamente for day trips or trips within 50 km.
2. Wrong or missing tjänsteställe, especially for hybrid and remote workers.
3. Meal reductions not applied: hotel breakfast, conference meals, customer dinners. Domestic breakfast reduction is 60 kr in 2026, not 45 kr.
4. Tremånadersregeln ignored, or breaks counted wrongly under SKV A 2025:5.
5. Tax-free milersättning in a förmånsbil where the employer paid part of the fuel. The 2026-07-01 exception covers plug-in hybrids only.
6. Reseräkningar missing times, purpose or kilometres.
7. Full VAT deducted on hyrbil, foreign VAT in the Swedish return, or representation VAT above 300 kr per person.
8. Bonuspoäng used privately and not reported.
9. A card statement used as the only underlag.

## 8. Travel policy recommendations

- Pay traktamente and milersättning at schablon, or state that anything above is taxed through payroll.
- Digital reseräkningar with timestamps and automatic meal reductions.
- Load Skatteverket's normalbelopp from its CSV/API, not by hand.
- Written rules for company cards and receipt deadlines (for example 5 working days).
- Ban private use of points earned on business travel, or require reporting.
- Full invoices in the company's name above 4 000 kr; hotels invoice the company directly.
- An annual EU VAT refund routine before 30 September.
- Register and review each employee's tjänsteställe once a year.

## 9. Per-trip checklist

- [ ] Business purpose documented; destination more than 50 km from both tjänsteställe and home
- [ ] At least one night (24:00-06:00) away if traktamente is paid
- [ ] Departure and return times recorded (12:00 / 19:00)
- [ ] Meals received recorded and reductions applied
- [ ] Three-month and two-year counters checked
- [ ] Correct country normalbelopp and day allocation for trips abroad
- [ ] Körjournal for mileage; förmånsbil fuel condition checked
- [ ] Receipts: förenklad faktura up to 4 000 kr, full invoice above
- [ ] VAT: 12 % hotel, 6 % transport, 50 % hyrbil, none on allowances, foreign VAT outside the Swedish return
- [ ] Foreign currency converted at the transaction-date rate or an approved approximation
- [ ] Representation booked separately with attendees and purpose
- [ ] Excess over schablon through payroll; crosses in AGI ruta 050 / 051
- [ ] Private use of bonuspoäng reported
- [ ] Verifikation complete; egen verifikation only as a last resort
- [ ] Archived for 7 years

## 10. Changes 2025-2026 and outlook 2027

**2026**
- Domestic traktamente 300 / 150 / 150 kr; 210 kr after three months.
- SKV A 2025:5 replaced SKV A 2023:14.
- Kostförmån 62 / 124 / 310 kr.
- Youth arbetsgivaravgifter 20,81 % (born 2003-2007, pay up to 25 000 kr/month) from 2026-04-01 to 2027-09-30.
- Food VAT 6 % from 2026-04-01; restaurant service stays at 12 %.
- From 2026-07-01: workplace charging permanently tax-free, and plug-in hybrid förmånsbilar charged at work keep 12 kr/mil.

**2027 (known or announced as of October 2026)**
- PBB 59 600 kr (set 2026-09-24). Domestic maximibelopp 0,5 % × 59 600 = 298, rounded to **300 kr**. Our calculation; confirm when Skatteverket publishes.
- 2027 normalbelopp and kostförmån not yet published.
- Food VAT returns to 12 % after 2027-12-31.
- The lagrådsremiss *Moderna och enklare skatteregler för arbetslivet* (September 2026, building on SOU 2025:4) proposes new rules for tjänsteställe, tjänsteresor and deductions for increased living costs. Watch for the commencement date.
