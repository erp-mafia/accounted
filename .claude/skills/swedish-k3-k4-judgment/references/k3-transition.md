# K3 transition: kapitel 35, switching from K2, and the 2025-2026 changes

Run a K2→K3 switch as a guided, human-approved workflow. The agent collects data, drafts the ingångsbalansräkning and the disclosures; a human approves the transition date, the component split, the deferred tax and every relief choice.

## Version control

BFN maintains several live K3 versions (BFN, "Vilken version av K-regelverken ska jag tillämpa?", 26 Aug 2025):

| Version | Applies to financial years beginning |
|---|---|
| 2023-12-12 | ≤ 2024-06-30 (normally årsredovisningar up to and including per 2025-05-31) |
| 2024-12-13 | 2024-07-01 to 2025-12-31 |
| 2025-06-16 (BFNAR 2025:3) | ≥ 2026-01-01 |
| consolidated 2025-12-15 | adds transitional points 3-4 to the 2025-06-16 text |

Select by **financial year start**. A company starting after 2025-06-30 with an extended first year ending 2026-12-31 or later applies the newest version immediately.

## What changed with BFNAR 2025:3 (decided 16 June 2025)

Mostly clarifications, with some substantive rules:
- scope (1.3) and guidance hierarchy (1.5)
- no comparatives in the vinstdisposition note (3.5)
- error correction via the opening balance for smaller companies (10.12, 10.14)
- gross presentation of own production in a kostnadsslagsindelad resultaträkning (5.4)
- annual result-based adjustment of HB/KB shares (9.9/14.12/15.16)
- rewritten tilläggsköpeskilling rules (19.8, 19.29)
- eventualtillgång recognised when "så gott som säkert" (21.15)
- group share-based payment (26.2-26.4)
- new kapitel 38 for bostadsrättsföreningar
- K2 1.1A-1.1C (BFNAR 2025:2) pushing more companies to K3

**Transitional points (December 2025, in the 2025-12-15 consolidation):**
- Point 2: a company forced off K2 may apply kapitel 35 even if it has applied K3 before.
- Point 3: the new group share-based payment rules apply to grants made from 2026-01-01.
- Point 4: larger existing K3 companies need not restate comparatives for principle changes caused by the 2025 amendments (e.g. 5.4, 19.8, 21.15) if they disclose the lack of comparability.

## Kapitel 35: first-time application

*(earlier numbering cross-checked with secondary sources; 35.18 confirmed current)*

**Scope.** 35.1 allows kapitel 35 to be used once, except for forced switchers (transitional point 2). A company returning to K3 after a period on K2 normally applies kapitel 10 (change of accounting principle) instead.

**Opening balance sheet.**
- Restate all statements and notes (35.2).
- Prepare an ingångsbalansräkning as of the tidpunkt för övergång (35.5-35.6).

**Transition date (35.7).**
- Main rule: the first day of the comparative year. A calendar-year company adopting K3 for 2026 has transition date 2025-01-01.
- A mindre företag that does not restate comparatives (ÅRL 3 kap. 5 §) uses the first day of the first K3 year: 2026-01-01. BFN notes the opening balance may be worked on during 2026.

**Retroactive application (35.8-35.11).** Main rule is retroactive application. Differences go directly to eget kapital in the ingångsbalansräkning (typically 2091 Balanserad vinst eller förlust).

**Mandatory exceptions (35.12-35.20).**
- Derecognised financial instruments are not re-recognised.
- Hedge accounting restrictions.
- **Estimates are not changed** (35.15): use the information available when the original estimates were made.
- **No capitalisation of previously expensed internal intangibles** (35.16).
- Goodwill amortisation is not restated (35.17).
- **Property, plant and equipment is not recalculated retroactively.** The transition-date carrying amount is split into significant components and depreciated over their remaining useful lives (35.18). Component splitting is therefore mandatory.
- Government grants (35.19).
- Borrowing costs (35.20).

**Voluntary reliefs (35.21-35.30).**
- Financial instruments may keep their previous treatment until disposal.
- Koncernbidrag need not be reclassified.
- Indirect manufacturing costs need not be added retroactively.
- Kapitel 19 need not be applied to business combinations before the transition date; restating one requires restating all later ones (35.24).
- Share-based payments.
- Accumulated translation differences may be reset to zero.
- Deferred tax may be omitted if it cannot be produced "utan oskälig kostnad" (35.27).
- Finance leases previously treated as operational.
- Dismantling costs.
- Impracticability (35.30).

**Legal entity.** Shares in subsidiaries, associates and JVs may keep their previous carrying amount (35.34).

**Disclosures.**
- That K3 is applied for the first time and which reliefs were used (35.31).
- Which accounting principles changed (35.33).
- For larger companies, a reconciliation of equity (35.33A).
- A mindre företag not restating comparatives discloses the lack of comparability.

## Worked example: fastighetsföretag moving to K3 for 2026 (BFN example)

- Building carrying amount at transition: 10 500 000. Skattemässigt värde: 9 500 000.
- Taxable temporary difference: 1 000 000. Deferred tax at 20.6 %: 206 000.
- Entry in the ingångsbalansräkning: Dr 2091 Balanserad vinst eller förlust 206 000 / Cr 2240 Avsättningar för uppskjutna skatter 206 000.
- Split the 1110/1119 balances into component sub-accounts (stomme, tak, fasad, stammar, installationer) based on the carrying amount; set remaining lives per component; update the anläggningsregister. Do **not** recompute historical depreciation.

## Data to collect in the year before the switch

- Anläggningsregister with acquisition values, accumulated depreciation, and a basis for the component split (property valuations, technical assessments).
- Skattemässiga värden for all assets and liabilities with temporary differences (buildings, development, provisions, untaxed reserves for group accounts).
- Contracts in progress and their stage of completion (if the group must use successiv vinstavräkning).
- Leasing contracts (needed for group accounts and for larger-company disclosures).
- Pension promises and their funding.

## Common switching errors (2026)

- No deferred tax in the ingångsbalansräkning.
- Retroactive recalculation of building depreciation (contrary to 35.18).
- Capitalising previously expensed development (contrary to 35.16).
- Missing first-time-application disclosures.
- Forgetting the 2089 Fond för utvecklingsutgifter when development is capitalised going forward.
