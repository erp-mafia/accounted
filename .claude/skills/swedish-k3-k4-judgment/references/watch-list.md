## Known gaps and caveats in this skill

- K3 point numbers for chapters 11, 13, 17, 27, 28, 30, 32 and most of 35 come from earlier consolidated texts plus secondary sources. 35.18 and 20.29 are confirmed current. Verify against vl12-1-k3-kons20251215.pdf before quoting.
- Chapters 11, 13, 30 and 32 are described at principle level only.
- The ÅRL 50/40/80 större företag thresholds and some IL references were not re-verified for 2026 in this research pass; `swedish-financial-reporting` states them as unchanged.
- The Bolagsverket rejection ranking relies on old statistics via a secondary source.
- RFR 2 is covered at a high level only.
