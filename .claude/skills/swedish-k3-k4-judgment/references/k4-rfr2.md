# K4: IFRS (koncern) and RFR 2 (juridisk person)

High-level only. **Agent rule:** handle legal-entity bookkeeping and the close under the client's documented RFR 2 policies. Escalate all IFRS consolidation work, IFRS 18 transition work, and any new RFR 2 policy choice. Read the full RFR 2 text before building deeper K4 automation.

## When IFRS applies

- Listed groups must use EU-IFRS in their koncernredovisning (Regulation (EC) 1606/2002). Unlisted groups may choose it.
- The legal entity always follows ÅRL. IFRS reaches it only through RFR 2.

## RFR 2 (January 2026)

Published 11 December 2025, replaces the January 2025 version, applies from financial years beginning 2026-01-01.

**Main rule.** The legal entity applies the IFRS used in the group, with exceptions where IFRS is incompatible with ÅRL, would give a deviating tax outcome (sambandet), or for other weighty reasons.

**Who.**
- Mandatory for listed parent companies.
- Optional under BFNAR 2012:3 for other legal entities included in IFRS group accounts.
- A company applying RFR 2 may not apply K3.

**Key exceptions.**
- IFRS 16 need not be applied: lease cost straight-line, as operational leases.
- IAS 19 defined benefit rules need not be applied: tryggandelagen instead.
- Obeskattade reserver are shown gross (no deferred tax split).
- Koncernbidrag may be reported as bokslutsdispositioner.
- Shares in subsidiaries at cost (less impairment).
- ÅRL balance sheet and income statement formats are kept.

## IFRS 18 (Presentation and Disclosure in Financial Statements)

- Endorsed 13 February 2026 (Commission Regulation (EU) 2026/338, OJ 16 February 2026). Effective for financial years beginning 2027-01-01.
- Groups need restated 2026 comparatives, so 2026 group reports should describe the expected transition effects.
- RFR 2 exempts the legal entity's income statement layout (ÅRL's format prevails) but allows IFRS 18 where it does not conflict with ÅRL. The balance sheet follows IFRS 18 as far as ÅRL allows.
- RFR 2 adds a voluntary relief from the management-defined performance measures (MPM) disclosures and an exception from the IAS 7 requirement to start the indirect cash flow statement from rörelseresultat. Both apply from 2027.

## Under review

- Removal of the RFR 2 exemption from component accounting for investment property under IAS 40 (FAR supportive, November 2025). Not decided: flag, do not apply.

## Interaction with tax

The RFR 2 exceptions exist mainly to preserve sambandet: legal-entity accounts drive the INK2 for timing questions (IL 14 kap. 2 §), and formal-link items (periodiseringsfond, överavskrivningar, koncernbidrag) must be booked to be deductible. A K4 group can therefore look very different from its parent's legal-entity accounts; never copy group IFRS figures into the legal entity's INK2 mapping.
