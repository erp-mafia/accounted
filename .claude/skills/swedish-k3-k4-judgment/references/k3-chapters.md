## 1. Scope and guidance hierarchy (kap 1)

- K3 applies to all companies applying ÅRL, except IFRS companies (1.3). It may not be used by companies that chose K2 or RFR 2, or that must apply RFR 2 under BFNAR 2012:3.
- Mandatory-K3 triggers: see SKILL.md, section 2.
- Where K3 has no rule (1.5): look first to similar K3 rules, then the kapitel 2 principles, then EU-IFRS together with RFR recommendations.
- Fusion accounting follows BFNAR 2020:5 (1.7).

## 2. Principles and presentation (kap 2-6)

**Principles.** ÅRL 2 kap. 3 § requires a rättvisande bild. ÅRL 2 kap. 4 § sets the basic principles: fortlevnad, konsekvens, försiktighet, periodisering, separate valuation, no netting. K3 kapitel 2 contains the definitions, the recognition test (2.18) and the requirement to account by economic substance.

**Comparatives (3.5).** Larger companies give comparatives in all notes except accounting principles and vinstdisposition. 3.6 requires disclosure of former name, entity or group, period, reporting currency and unit.

**Formats.** Follow ÅRL bilagor 2-3. In a kostnadsslagsindelad resultaträkning, own production is presented gross, with lagerförändring or aktiverat arbete för egen räkning (BAS 38xx) as income (5.4, new 2026).

**Eget kapital (kap 6).** Changes in equity and, in the legal entity, aktieägartillskott and koncernbidrag (6.3-6.5).

**Koncernbidrag.**
- Given: Dr 8830 Lämnade koncernbidrag / Cr 2860 Skulder till koncernföretag
- Received: Dr 1660 Fordringar hos koncernföretag / Cr 8820 Mottagna koncernbidrag
- Tax requires IL 35 kap. conditions (more than 90 % ownership for the whole year). Escalate if ownership changed during the year.

**Materiality.** Document the thresholds used. Never use materiality to omit legally required disclosures or to tolerate known errors.

## 3. Kassaflödesanalys (kap 7)

- Required for **larger companies and bostadsrättsföreningar** (ÅRL 2 kap. 1 §), and always in koncernredovisning. Kapitel 7 also governs voluntary statements (7.1).
- Likvida medel (7.4): cash, available bank balances, and short-term liquid investments with insignificant value risk. A group cash-pool share may be included; for a brf, the client-funds account held by the förvaltare (38.16).
- Practice: indirect method. Start from resultat efter finansiella poster, adjust for non-cash items (78xx avskrivningar, 77xx nedskrivningar, change in avsättningar 22xx, unrealised FX), then working capital. Investing flows: 10xx-13xx. Financing flows: 23xx borrowings, utdelning, aktieägartillskott.
- Pending: SOU 2026:59 would remove the requirement for unlisted medelstora företag from 2028. Not law.

## 4. Immateriella tillgångar (kap 18) and goodwill (kap 19)

**Model choice.** Choose the **kostnadsföringsmodellen** (18.8) or the **aktiveringsmodellen** (18.7, 18.9-18.12). Under the aktiveringsmodellen research is always expensed; development is capitalised only when all 18.12 criteria are demonstrated: technical feasibility, intention to complete, ability to use or sell, probable future economic benefits, adequate resources, and reliable measurement of the expenditure. The choice applies consistently; acquired parts follow the chosen model (18.13A). The legal entity may expense even if the group capitalises (18.30).

**Fond för utvecklingsutgifter (ÅRL 4 kap. 2 §).** An AB or ekonomisk förening that capitalises development must transfer the same amount to bundet eget kapital, even with an accumulated loss (18.3A).
- Capitalise: Dr 1010 Utvecklingsutgifter (101x sub-accounts) / Cr 38xx Aktiverat arbete för egen räkning (or Cr the cost accounts the expenditure was booked on)
- Transfer to fund: Dr 2091 Balanserad vinst eller förlust / Cr 2089 Fond för utvecklingsutgifter
- Release as the asset is amortised or written down: Dr 2089 / Cr 2091

**Useful life.** If it cannot be reliably estimated, use ten years (ÅRL 4 kap. 4 §).

**Goodwill and tilläggsköpeskilling.** Tilläggsköpeskilling is included in the acquisition value when probable and reliably measurable (19.8). Later changes go to the P&L in the group but adjust the share acquisition value in the legal entity (19.29, 21.22). Goodwill impairment is never reversed.

**Tax.** Development expenditure is deductible when incurred, so capitalising creates a taxable temporary difference (deferred tax in the group, and in a K3 legal entity under kap 29).

**K2 contrast.** K2 prohibits capitalising internally generated intangibles.

**Escalate:** first capitalisation, a model switch, impairment.

## 5. Materiella anläggningstillgångar (kap 17)

*(earlier numbering)*
- **Components (17.4).** If the consumption pattern of significant components differs materially, the asset is split and each component depreciated separately. Replacements of a component are capitalised and the replaced part derecognised (17.5, 17.21A). Without components, significant subsequent expenditure that meets 2.18 is capitalised (17.5A).
- **Indirect costs** are included in cost if more than insignificant (17.6).
- **Residual value and useful life** are estimated at the acquisition price level and reviewed when there is an indication of change (17.14-17.17).
- **Depreciation** starts when the asset is available for use, continues while it is idle, and reflects consumption (17.18-17.20).
- **Legal entity reliefs (17.24-17.26).** Assets of low value or with a useful life of 3 years or less may be expensed if the tax deduction is also taken immediately. Gifts may be carried at tax value. Dismantling costs may be excluded from cost.

**Typical buildings components:** stomme, tak, fasad, stammar, installationer (el, VVS, ventilation), hiss, markanläggning. Use sub-accounts of 1110/1119 and record lives in the anläggningsregister.

**Entries.**
- Depreciation per component: Dr 7820 Avskrivningar på byggnader och markanläggningar / Cr 1119
- Equipment: Dr 7830 Avskrivningar på maskiner och inventarier / Cr 1229
- Överavskrivningar (legal entity): Dr 8850 Förändring av överavskrivningar / Cr 2150 Ackumulerade överavskrivningar

**Tax.** Building tax depreciation follows IL 19 kap. regardless of components (no formal link). Räkenskapsenlig avskrivning for inventarier (IL 18 kap. 13 §) requires the books to match (formal link).

**Escalate:** building and plant component split, residual values, changes in useful life, capitalising borrowing costs.

## 6. Nedskrivningar (kap 27)

*(earlier numbering; indicator points and återvinningsvärde definition not verified in the 2025-12-15 text)*
- **Scope (27.1).** Anläggningstillgångar, except deferred tax assets, employee-benefit assets and financial assets other than shares in subsidiaries, associates and JVs.
- **Indicators.** At each balance sheet date, assess: market value decline beyond normal use, adverse technological, market, economic or legal change, increased market interest rates, obsolescence or physical damage, restructuring or discontinuation, and worse-than-expected performance.
- **Measurement.** Compare carrying amount with återvinningsvärde = the higher of verkligt värde less selling costs (27.6) and nyttjandevärde (present value of future cash flows). Within a kassagenererande enhet, no individual asset is written below the highest of its verkligt värde less selling costs, its nyttjandevärde and zero (27.13).
- **Reversal** is capped at the carrying amount that would have existed without the impairment (27.15-27.16). Goodwill is never reversed (27.17) and is allocated to kassagenererande enheter (27.18).
- **ÅRL 4 kap. 5 §.** Anläggningstillgångar must be written down if the decline is expected to be bestående. Financial fixed assets *may* be written down even if the decline is not lasting.

**Entries.** Dr 7710 Nedskrivningar av immateriella anläggningstillgångar / 7720 … materiella anläggningstillgångar / 8070 or 8170 (financial) against the asset or its accumulated impairment account (e.g. 1018, 1118, 1228, 1318).

**Tax.** Impairment of näringsbetingade andelar is not deductible (IL 25 a kap.).

**Always escalate.** The agent lists indicators with evidence; a human sets the amount.

## 7. Finansiella instrument (kap 11 and 12)

- **Choice.** Kapitel 11 (cost-based) or kapitel 12 (fair value under ÅRL 4 kap. 14 a-e §§). Kapitel 12 applies to all instruments except those listed in 12.2. Companies using ÅRL 4 kap. 14 b § third paragraph apply EU-IFRS to all instruments. **Kapitel 12 may not be used in the legal entity if the group uses kapitel 11 (12.1).** Embedded derivatives are separated under 12.76.
- **Kapitel 11 in practice.** Current assets at the lower of cost and fair value (lägsta värdets princip); portfolio valuation permitted for a securities portfolio. Financial fixed assets written down on lasting decline. Hedge accounting requires documentation at inception.
- **Osäkra kundfordringar.**
  - Reclassify: Dr 1515 Osäkra kundfordringar / Cr 1510 Kundfordringar
  - Reserve: Dr 6352 Befarade kundförluster / Cr 1519 Nedskrivning av kundfordringar
  - Confirmed loss: Dr 6351 Konstaterade kundförluster / Cr 1515 (reverse the 1519 reserve), adjust utgående moms per ML if applicable
- **Tax.** Reserves for doubtful receivables made under god redovisningssed are normally deductible.

**Escalate:** kapitel 12, hedging, derivatives, convertibles, loans to and from group companies or owners (check ABL 21 kap. förbjudna lån).

## 8. Varulager (kap 13)

- Cost includes indirect manufacturing costs. Use FIFO or weighted average (no LIFO). Lägsta värdets princip applies (ÅRL 4 kap. 9 §): the lower of cost and nettoförsäljningsvärde.
- Entry: Dr/Cr 1460 Lager av handelsvaror (14xx) against 4990 Lagerförändring (or 4900-series).
- Tax generally follows the books (IL 17 kap.). The 97 % schablon is a tax alternative; it is not a reason to understate the accounting value.

**Escalate:** significant count differences, obsolescence write-downs, overhead allocation changes.

## 9. Leasing (kap 20)

- **Scope (20.1).** All leases, except licence agreements and onerous operating leases (those go to kapitel 21).
- **Group.** Finance leases are capitalised as an asset and a liability.
- **Legal entity (20.29).** "I juridisk person får alla finansiella leasingavtal redovisas som operationella leasingavtal". Larger companies still give the operating-lease disclosures (future minimum lease payments).

**Entries.**
- Legal entity, operational: Dr 5615 Leasing av personbilar / 5220 Hyra av inventarier / Cr 2440 Leverantörsskulder
- Group, finance lease: Dr 1220 / Cr 2350 at inception; then depreciation (7830), interest (8410) and amortisation of the liability.

**K2 contrast.** K2 treats all leases as operational; the K3 legal-entity relief mirrors that and tax.

## 10. Intäkter (kap 23)

- **Identification (23.7).** Each transaction is accounted for separately; split it into parts where needed to reflect substance, and combine transactions that form one business event.
- **Varuförsäljning.** Recognise when the significant risks and rewards have transferred and the seller has no continuing involvement or control.
- **Tjänste- och entreprenaduppdrag.**
  - Löpande räkning: income at the value of work performed and material delivered (23.17).
  - Fast pris: income by stage of completion (successiv vinstavräkning), estimates revised at each balance sheet date (23.21). Expected losses are recognised immediately.
- **Legal entity (23.31-23.37).** Pågående arbeten in industries covered by IL 17 kap. 23 § (bygg, anläggning, hantverk, konsult) may use the **färdigställandemetod**. Changing to successiv vinstavräkning is allowed; changing back requires särskilda skäl. **The group must use successiv vinstavräkning.**

**Entries.**
- Successiv vinstavräkning: Dr 1620 Upparbetad men ej fakturerad intäkt / Cr 30xx, or Dr 30xx / Cr 2450 Fakturerad men ej upparbetad intäkt
- Färdigställandemetod: Dr 1470 Pågående arbeten / Cr 4970 Förändring av pågående arbeten

**Tax.** Skatteverket (SKV 294) follows the method used in the books when it complies with god redovisningssed.

**Escalate:** stage-of-completion estimates, loss contracts, multi-element contracts, principal vs agent.

## 11. Avsättningar och eventualförpliktelser (kap 21)

- **Scope (21.1).** Excludes executory contracts, leases, entreprenad, employee benefits and income tax; includes onerous operating leases.
- **Recognition.** Present obligation (legal or informal) from a past event, probable outflow, reliable estimate. Measure at the best estimate; discount if the effect is material.
- **Eventualförpliktelser** are disclosed, not recognised.
- **Eventualtillgång.** Recognise an asset when the inflow is "så gott som säkert" (21.15, new 2026).
- **Legal entity.** Contingent consideration adjustments follow 19.29 (21.22).

**Entries.** Dr 6990 / relevant 7xxx cost account / Cr 2220 Avsättningar för garantier / 2290 Övriga avsättningar.

**Tax.** Many provisions are not deductible until paid (IL 16 kap.), creating a deductible temporary difference.

**Escalate:** litigation, environmental and restructuring provisions, contingent assets.

## 12. Inkomstskatter (kap 29)

- Tax expense = current tax + deferred tax, measured at the enacted rate (20.6 %).
- Deferred tax assets on tax losses are recognised only if it is probable they can be used.
- Classification: deferred tax assets as financial fixed assets, deferred tax liabilities as avsättningar. Netting only with a legal right of offset (29.32).
- No deferred tax on temporary differences in subsidiary, associate or JV holdings where the company controls the timing (29.20).
- **Legal entity (29.37).** The deferred tax liability attributable to obeskattade reserver is not shown separately. Reserves are gross in the balance sheet and bokslutsdispositioner gross in the P&L.
- **Group.** Obeskattade reserver are split into 20.6 % deferred tax and 79.4 % equity.

**Entries.**
- Taxable temporary difference: Dr 8940 Uppskjuten skatt / Cr 2240 Avsättningar för uppskjutna skatter
- Deductible temporary difference: Dr 1370 Uppskjuten skattefordran / Cr 8940
- Group elimination of 1 000 in periodiseringsfond (2110): Dr 2110 1 000 / Cr 2240 206 / Cr eget kapital 794

**K2 contrast.** K2 prohibits deferred tax.

**Escalate:** deferred tax assets on losses, IL 40 kap. ownership-change limitations, uncertain tax positions.

## 13. Ersättningar till anställda / pensioner (kap 28)

*(earlier numbering)*
- **Avgiftsbestämda planer** are expensed as earned (28.13).
- **Lättnadsregel (28.14 b, 28.18).** A förmånsbestämd plan is treated as avgiftsbestämd when a premium is paid to an insurer, e.g. ITP 2 via Alecta.
- **Pension in own regi** requires an external calculation, e.g. PRI.
- **Promises tied to kapitalförsäkring** are measured at the carrying amount of the asset (28.12A).
- 28.22 concerns foreign subsidiaries in the group; tryggandelagen rules for the legal entity are in 28.31-28.32.

**Entries.**
- Premiums: Dr 7410 Pensionsförsäkringspremier / Cr 2990 (or 2440)
- Särskild löneskatt 24.26 %: Dr 7533 Särskild löneskatt för pensionskostnader / Cr 2514 Beräknad särskild löneskatt på pensionskostnader
- Kapitalförsäkring: Dr 1385 Värde av kapitalförsäkring / Cr 1930, then Dr 7420 / Cr 2230 Övriga avsättningar för pensioner
- Tryggandelagen own-regi: Cr 2210 Avsättningar för pensioner enligt tryggandelagen

**Tax.** Deductions follow IL 28 kap., not the books.

**Escalate:** own-regi förmånsbestämda planer, owner pensions.

## 14. Koncern, intresseföretag, joint ventures (kap 9, 14, 15, 19)

- The consolidation obligation follows ÅRL 7 kap., with exemptions for mindre koncerner and for subsidiaries consolidated by an EEA parent. (SOU 2026:59 would extend the exemption to equivalent third-country accounts; not law.)
- Förvärvsmetoden applies, except for common-control transactions and JV formation (19.1).
- Intresseföretag: equity method in the group, cost in the legal entity (14.12-14.14).
- **New for 2026:** HB/KB shares in the legal entity are adjusted annually by the owner's share of the HB result, with a liability if the share turns negative (9.9/14.12/15.16).

**Always escalate.**

## 15. Utländsk valuta (kap 30)

- Transactions at the transaction-date rate. Monetary items are translated at the closing rate.
- Operating exchange differences: 3960 Valutakursvinster / 7960 Valutakursförluster. Financial: 8330 / 8430.
- Group: current-rate method, with an omräkningsreserv in equity.
- Tax generally follows closing-rate translation.

## 16. Händelser efter balansdagen (kap 32)

- Adjusting events that confirm conditions at the balance sheet date are reflected (e.g. a customer's bankruptcy, a court ruling).
- Material non-adjusting events are disclosed in a note.
- A proposed dividend is not a liability at the balance sheet date.
- Going-concern doubts arising after year end override the fortlevnad assumption.
- Keep an events log up to the signing date (ÅRL 2 kap. 7 §).

## 17. Närstående (kap 33) and noter

**Related parties.** Larger companies disclose related-party relationships and material transactions not on market terms. All companies disclose loans and guarantees to board members and the CEO (ÅRL 5 kap.). Agents should tag owner, board, relative and group counterparties and route them for human review.

**Notes beyond K2 typically include:** significant judgments and estimates; revenue by category; fixed-asset roll-forwards; tax reconciliation (larger companies); deferred tax by type; borrowings and maturities; securities pledged and contingencies; leases; related parties; events after the balance sheet date; vinstdisposition; the first-time-application note (kapitel 35).

## 18. Bostadsrättsföreningar (kap 38)

- From financial years beginning after 2025-12-31 all bostadsrättsföreningar and bostadsföreningar apply K3 with kapitel 38 (BFNAR 2023:1 is repealed when kapitel 38 applies). Bostadsaktiebolag are not covered.
- Kassaflödesanalys is required.
- The underhållsfond is bundet eget kapital; transfers are made on decision (38.14-38.15).
- Förvaltningsberättelse content: 38.2-38.12, including additional key ratios (38.3-38.11).
- Disclose the content of nettoomsättning and årsavgifter (38.17).
- Always audited.
