## Already in effect

### 1 July 2024: Pappersfritt bokföring (SFS 2024:342)
**BFL 7 kap 1-7 §§ amended.**
- No longer required to keep paper originals after digitization (7 kap 6§)
- Applies retroactively to material digitized before 1 July 2024
- The digital copy must be a faithful reproduction
- Must still preserve for 7 years
- Electronic documents must be kept in the format and with the content they had when received or compiled (7 kap 1§ 3 st)
- Electronic records may be stored in another EU country, or a non-EU country with equivalent mutual-assistance instruments, if the location is reported to Skatteverket, authorities get immediate electronic access and a printout can be made in Sweden (7 kap 3a§); otherwise a permit is needed (7 kap 4§)
- BFNAR 2024:1 (applies from 16 September 2024) updated BFNAR 2013:2 to match: backups stored separately from the original (8.2); downloaded and self-created information saved in the format in which it is meant to be presented (7.3, 7.5); a paper document kept abroad may be destroyed there once its information has been transferred (8.5A)
- **Software implication**: receipt OCR/scanning is now the primary archival path, not supplementary. Your digitization workflow must ensure completeness and immutability.

### 1 January 2025: Momsregistreringsgräns höjd
- Threshold raised from **80 000 kr → 120 000 kr** (ML 18 kap 4§, Lag 2024:942, prop. 2023/24:149, riksdagsbeslut hösten 2024). Measured per calendar year: the exemption applies if årsomsättningen inom landet does not exceed 120 000 kr in the current calendar year **and** did not exceed it in either of the two preceding calendar years. It ends from the point the limit is exceeded during the year (18 kap 11§). Den **80 000 kr-gränsen var den föregående höjningen 1 juli 2022** (från 30 000 kr): inte den nuvarande.
- Companies within these limits need not register for moms (an already registered company must apply to Skatteverket to use the exemption, 18 kap 9§)
- Can still register voluntarily
- **Software implication**: update validation logic for momsregistrering recommendations to use 120 000 kr threshold from 2025-01-01.

### 1 January 2025: AGI föräldraledighet/VAB
- Employers must report monthly to Skatteverket when employees take föräldraledighet or vård av sjukt barn (VAB)
- Reported as part of arbetsgivardeklaration
- **Software implication**: add föräldraledighet/VAB fields to AGI submission.

## Effective 1 January 2026

### 3:12-reglerna reformed
Major overhaul of fåmansföretagsreglerna:

**New gränsbelopp calculation (3 components)** per SFS 2025:1361:
1. Grundbelopp: **4 IBB av föregående inkomstår** = för inkomstår 2026: 4 × IBB(2025) = **4 × 80 600 = 322 400 kr**, divided equally among shares. (För inkomstår 2027 används IBB 2026 = 83 400.)
2. Lönebaserat utrymme: 50 % × (delägarens andel av löneunderlag − 8 IBB per delägare). 8 IBB beräknas på samma föregående år som grundbeloppet: för inkomstår 2026 är 8 IBB = 8 × 80 600 = **644 800 kr per delägare**. Max: 50 × egen/närstående lön.
3. Ränta på omkostnadsbelopp: statslåneränta + 9 % on omkostnadsbelopp > 100 000 kr. För inkomstår 2026: SLR 2,55 % + 9 % = 11,55 %.

**Key changes:**
- Löneuttagskravet slopat (no minimum salary requirement for inkomstår 2026)
- Replaced by löneavdrag: 8 IBB per delägare subtracted från delägarens andel av löneunderlaget
- 4-procentsspärren borta: all delägare can use löneunderlag regardless of ownership share
- Karenstid shortened from 5 to 4 years
- Ränteuppräkning of sparat utdelningsutrymme abolished
- Index- och kapitalunderlagsreglerna phased out (can still be used until 2029 for old shares)

**Software implication**: K10-blanketten for inkomstår 2026 (filed spring 2027) uses new rules. If your software calculates utdelningsutrymme, the entire calculation engine needs rebuilding.

### K2/K3 changes (BFN decision June 2025)
Applies to räkenskapsår starting after 31 December 2025 (i.e., 2026 calendar year onwards). Nystartade företag starting after 30 June 2025 with förlängt first räkenskapsår ending 31 Dec 2026 or later must apply new rules immediately.

**Excluded from K2 regardless of size (must use K3):**
- Bostadsrättsföreningar and bostadsföreningar
- Companies with one or more filialer abroad
- Companies with direct holdings of kryptotillgångar (occasional use as a means of payment excepted)
- Companies that acquired goods or services against aktierelaterade ersättningar
- Companies with issued skuldebrev that can be settled in egetkapitalinstrument, or similar sammansatta finansiella instrument

**Excluded only for companies outside the lättnadsregel** (those exceeding more than one of: > 3 anställda, > 1.5 MSEK balansomslutning, > 3 MSEK nettoomsättning, in each of the last two years):
- Byggnader generate >= 75% of nettoomsättning
- A material uppskjuten skatteskuld
- A company already on K2 that is only occasionally caught by these two may stay on K2

**Other K2 changes (BFNAR 2025:2):**
- Periodisering: gränsbeloppet raised to 7 000 kr per post (2.4, previously 5 000 kr); förskott under 7 000 kr (2.4A); no simplification if the combined effect is material (2.4B). The rule for recurring costs (7.9) remains separate
- Uppställningsformer: posten Råvaror och förnödenheter clarified and widened (4.4); new balance-sheet post Övriga immateriella anläggningstillgångar for tomträtter; Färdiga varor och handelsvaror split into two posts; Checkräkningskredit renamed Kontokredit (4.7)
- Tilläggsköpeskilling on acquisition and sale of inkråm and andelar (6.32A, 8.4B, 9.3A)
- Price adjustments on hyra and leasing, e.g. hyresrabatter (6.26, 7.10)
- Uttag ur kapitalförsäkring (8.4C, 11.13A)
- Two ways to report rättelse av fel (2.12)
- Pågående arbeten för annans räkning: uppdrag with a positive balance may not be netted against uppdrag with a negative balance (6.24)
- Transition: a new valuation method applies only to new transactions and to posts revalued at each balansdag; comparatives are not restated

**K3 changes:**
- Updated rules for aktierelaterade ersättningar (ch. 26)
- Updated rules for eventualtillgångar (ch. 21.15): must now be "så gott som säkert" for recognition
- New transitional provisions for applying changes

**Software implication**: if your system helps companies choose K-regelverk, update the decision logic. If you generate årsredovisning, BRF templates need K3 format.

### Ålderspensionsavgift
- Persons who are 67+ at year start: only ålderspensionsavgift 10.21%. For 2026 that is born 1938-1958 (the same cohort as in 2025, when the threshold was 66)
- Born 1937 or earlier: no arbetsgivaravgifter
- Total rate stays 31.42%, but the components change from 2026 (Lag 2025:1362): föräldraförsäkringsavgift 2.00%, efterlevandepensionsavgift 0.30%, arbetsskadeavgift 0.10%, allmän löneavgift 12.62%

### F-skatt changes
- Applicant can request tidsbegränsat godkännande
- Skatteverket gains right to demand documentation proving eligibility
- Intended to combat missbruk of F-skatt

### ROT-avdrag
- Returns to 30 % of arbetskostnad fr.o.m. 2026-01-01 (was temporarily **50 %** under perioden 12 maj 2025-31 december 2025 enligt prop. 2024/25:156; ej 35 % som tidigare angetts).
- Max 50 000 kr per person per year: taket var oförändrat 50 000 kr även under den tillfälliga 50 %-perioden 2025, bara subventionsgraden höjdes (30 → 50 %). Det höjda/separerade taket på 75 000 kr ROT gällde endast 2024 (prop. 2023/24:86).
- Combined ROT+RUT max 2026: 75 000 kr

### Reseavdrag
- Threshold for avdrag for resor between bostad och arbetsplats raised from 11 000 to 15 000 kr

### CSRD hållbarhetsrapportering
- Delayed two years (Lag 2025:1297, "stop-the-clock"): large companies not already reporting (wave 2) start with räkenskapsår beginning after 31 December 2026 (reporting year 2027)
- Criteria (ÅRL 6 kap 10§): more than one of, in each of the last two years: > 250 employees, > 280 MSEK balansomslutning, > 550 MSEK nettoomsättning
- Listed SMEs: räkenskapsår beginning after 31 December 2027
- **Proposed only** (SOU 2026:27, implementing the EU Omnibus): narrow the requirement to companies with > 1 000 employees and > 4.9 billion kr nettoomsättning. Not enacted
- **Software implication**: not directly accounting, but some customers may need help with data collection

## Effective 1 April 2026

### Sänkt livsmedelsmoms (Prop. 2025/26:55, SFS 2026:118)
- Livsmedel: 12% -> 6% (tillfälligt)
- Period: 1 April 2026 - 31 December 2027
- Restaurang/servering: stays at 12%
- Transition rule: rate based on leveransdatum, not fakturadatum
- Take-away/avhämtning: 6%. Servering/förtäring på plats: 12%

**Critical implementation details:**
- Invoices spanning the transition date: split by delivery date
- Advance payments (förskott): the beskattningsgrundande händelse is when the payment is received (ML 7 kap 7§), so a förskott received before 1 April 2026 keeps 12% even if delivery happens later
- Credit notes: apply the rate that was valid for the original transaction
- BAS accounts: utgående moms on livsmedel sales moves from 2621 (12%) to 2631 (6%), and sales from 3002 to 3003. EU purchases: 2624 -> 2634 and 4516 -> 4517. Import: 2625 -> 2635
- Momsdeklaration: utgående moms on domestic sales moves from ruta 11 (12%) to ruta 12 (6%)

**Software implication**: your system needs a date-aware momssats lookup. Hard-coding rates is not viable. Store momssatser with giltighetstid (valid_from, valid_to).

### Tillfälligt nedsatta arbetsgivaravgifter för unga (Lag 2026:100)
- Applies to ersättning paid 1 April 2026-30 September 2027 to employees who at year start have turned 18 but not 23 (2026: born 2003-2007)
- 20.81% (ålderspensionsavgift plus half of the other avgifter) on ersättning up to 25 000 kr per calendar month; full 31.42% on the excess
- **Software implication**: split each eligible employee's avgiftsunderlag at 25 000 kr per month in the AGI, based on birth year and payment date.

## Effective 1 July 2026

### Skatteverket digital granskning (Prop. 2025/26:107)
- In force 1 July 2026 (riksdagsbeslut 25 March 2026, bet. 2025/26:SkU11)
- Skatteverket may access digital bokföring directly via internet during revision
- Only when legal ground for kontroll already exists
- Removes the ban on telenät-based granskning in skatteförfarandelagen

**Software implication**: your system should support read-only access for Skatteverket during revision. Accounted's immutable `audit_log`, WORM document storage, and immutability of posted entries naturally support this. Consider building a dedicated API endpoint or export mechanism.

### Sänkt moms på danstillställningar (Prop. 2025/26:109, SFS 2026:841)
- Tillträde till danstillställningar: 25% -> 6% (ML 9 kap 14§), from 1 July 2026

## On the horizon (2027+)

### Kassaregister (enacted)
From 1 January 2027 every kassaregister must meet Skatteverket's requirements in SKVFS 2021:17. A journalminne must be able to produce its registrations in XML format.

### Obligatorisk e-fakturering B2B
EU ViDA (VAT in the Digital Age, Council Directive (EU) 2025/516) is adopted. Platforms for short-term accommodation rental and passenger transport by road become deemed suppliers from 1 July 2028. Structured e-invoicing with digital reporting for cross-border B2B transactions, replacing periodisk sammanställning, applies from 1 July 2030. A domestic B2B mandate in Sweden is **not decided**: a Swedish inquiry reports by 30 November 2027. Standards: Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, EN 16931.

**Software implication**: start supporting Peppol e-invoicing format now. It's already used by the public sector (offentlig sektor requires e-faktura since 2019). The 2030 EU requirement covers cross-border B2B; a domestic mandate would make it universal.

### Livsmedelsmoms reversion
Enacted (SFS 2026:119): from 1 January 2028 livsmedel reverts from 6% to 12%. Your system must handle the reversion date. Make sure momssats lookup is date-driven.

### Enacted vs proposed (status September 2026)
- **Enacted:** BFL 7 kap 1§ format retention and 7 kap 3a§ storage abroad (SFS 2024:342, in force 1 July 2024); BFNAR 2024:1 (from 16 September 2024); K2 changes BFNAR 2025:2 (räkenskapsår beginning after 31 December 2025); Skatteverket digital granskning (1 July 2026); kassaregister under SKVFS 2021:17 (1 January 2027); livsmedel back to 12% (1 January 2028); ViDA (1 July 2028 / 1 July 2030)
- **Proposed only:** CSRD Omnibus thresholds (SOU 2026:27); domestic B2B e-invoicing (inquiry due 30 November 2027)
