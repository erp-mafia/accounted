# Design Context & Design System (Stil v3)

The conventions below are locked: deviating from them on existing pages is a regression. Stil v3 (founder decisions 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-01, canvas "Stil v3") replaced the editorial-monochrome system of 2026-07: pills became 6px rectangles, Hedvig moved to headline numbers only, Inter became the one text face, and cards inside the panel are gone.

## Users

Swedish sole traders (enskild firma) and small business owners (aktiebolag) who manage their own bookkeeping. They are not accountants; they are professionals (consultants, freelancers, shop owners) who want to stay compliant without hiring one. They use Accounted in short, focused sessions: sending an invoice, categorizing bank transactions, filing a VAT declaration. Speed and clarity matter.

## Brand & Aesthetic

**Quiet instrument, with warmth.** The affectUI "clean" language (free MIT components) adapted to Accounted: a warm frame, a white panel, a warm ink scale, hairline shadows instead of borders, 6px controls, Inter everywhere and Hedvig Letters Serif only for the big numbers. Anti-references: enterprise density, neon SaaS coldness, boxes inside boxes.

- **Palette**: a warm ink rgb(28 24 18) in tiers on a white panel over the warm frame `#F5F3EE` (`--frame`). Text tokens are opaque: `--strong` `#17140F` (titles, amounts, the active choice), `--foreground` ink .90 (body, values), `text-foreground/80` ink .72 (secondary text, ghost buttons), `--muted-foreground` ink .62 (the lowest text tier: AA on the panel 4.9:1 and on the frame 4.8:1; affectUI's .45 is never used). Washes and lines are translucent tokens (alpha inside the triplet): `--secondary` warm wash .10 (selected), `bg-secondary/60` (.06, hover), `bg-secondary/35` (.035, row hover), `--muted` .07, `--line` .08 (dividers), `--line-strong` .18 (totals), `--border` .10 (the outline of a single object). Raw CSS that adds alpha to a translucent token uses `color-mix()`, never `hsl(var(--x) / a)`. Status colors: green `--success` `#1F7A51`, amber `--warning` `#D4930F` (glyphs and bars, never small text) with `--attn` `#8A5D04` for amber text, red `--destructive` `#B23A27`, blue `--ring` for keyboard focus. They are data, not chrome (convention 12).
- **Dark mode**: neutral grey (dark drops the warm tint). Four surface steps: frame `#0D0D0D`, panel `#1C1C1E`, floating layer `#262628` (`--popover`), raised control `#2C2C2F` (`--control`); white ink .94 / .76 / .64, never below .60; the primary button becomes a light chip with dark text; status colors brightened (`#3EC27A` / `#52CF8C`, `#F0B232`, `#FF7B6E`); focus ring at .75. Every text tier passes AA on the panel and on a floating layer.
- **Typography**: Inter for everything (`--font-body`), Geist Mono for account numbers and code, Hedvig Letters Serif (`font-display`) for headline numbers only (24/28/32px). Hedvig is single-weight (400): never `font-medium` on it. The type scale is Tailwind's named sizes, redefined in `app/globals.css`: `text-xs` 12/16 (labels, meta), `text-sm` 13/20 (body, rows, buttons, the body default), `text-base` and `text-lg` 15/22 (titles: dialog, section, drawer), `text-xl` 20/28 (the rare page heading), `text-2xl`/`3xl`/`4xl` 24/28/32 (Hedvig numbers). Titles are Inter 500 in the strong ink with -0.006em tracking. Every label is 12px/500 in sentence case: no uppercase, no tracked capitals. Tabular numbers everywhere financial data appears; dates in lists use proportional figures (`.date-figures`).
- **Surfaces**: the page is a rounded panel (12px) floating on the frame with the panel hairline shadow (`shadow-panel`), no border. **No cards inside the panel**: the full-bleed panel is already the card, so lists, tables, report sections and settings sit directly on it, divided by hairlines (`border-b-hairline`, 0.5px), with list cells 8px in and an 8px bleed (`LIST_BLEED_CLASS`, `-mx-2`) so text stays on the 24px gutter while hover tints and hairlines reach past it; ledger tables (`VTH_CLASS`/`VTD_CLASS`) have no left padding and never bleed. Hem's panes sit on the gutter with a 4px row inset. Only floating layers (dialogs, drawers, menus, popovers, floating bars, toasts) get a surface (`bg-popover`) and the float shadow (`shadow-float`). A single object (a file, a bank row to choose, a plan to pick) gets the raised control surface (`Card variant="object"`: `bg-control shadow-raised rounded-lg`).
- **Shadows**: `shadow-raised` (`--sh`: a 0.5px ink ring plus a 1-2px lift) on every raised control and single object, `shadow-raised-hover` on hover, `shadow-float` (`--sh-pop`) on floating layers, `shadow-drawer` on drawers, `shadow-panel` on the page panel. Tailwind's own `shadow-sm` to `shadow-2xl` are banned.
- **Frosted chrome**: the top bar and floating bars are material (`--material`: 72% of the surface + 20px blur), no hard divider; reduced transparency (`reduced-transparency:` variant, `prefers-reduced-transparency`) gets the solid surface.
- **Spacing**: a 24px gutter (16px on a phone), 16px inset inside floating layers' sections, 20px insets in dialogs and drawers. Dense data uses two row heights only: 44px for lists, 36px for ledger tables.
- **Motion**: functional, with four named motions allowed (founder decision 2026-10-29): **press scale** (buttons and chips scale to .97 on pointer down over `duration-100`, the Button primitive owns it), **fold away** (a row leaves with `.row-exit`), **check draw** (the confirmation chip's check, `.check-draw`), **rolling counter** (a count that changes rolls its digits, `.roll-digit`). Nothing else moves on hover. Three speeds: `duration-100` (press), `duration-150` (state changes: color, opacity, chevrons, toggles), `duration-300` (layout changes: expand/collapse, drawers, content entry). Curves are named: `ease-out` (state), `ease-emphasized` (entry, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`), `ease-drawer` (slide-overs, sheets, the panel resize); never paste a `cubic-bezier` literal. Content entry: `.stagger-enter` for lists/tables (3px rise with a 2px blur that clears, 30ms steps), `animate-rise` for a single block; `animate-slide-up` is the auth pages' entry only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (wired globally; the icon adapter stills icons too). The icon exception stays: an icon plays its own short Heroicons animation while its link, button or menu row is hovered.
- **Icons**: Heroicons (24px outline, 1.5 stroke), animated, imported only from `@/components/icons` (lint fails `lucide-react` or a direct `@heroicons-animated/react` import); a missing icon is added there. Size and color with `className` (`h-4 w-4`, `text-muted-foreground`): 16px in sidebar rows, 20px on the rail and in state blocks. Status reads through `StatusGlyph` (a filled circle with the mark cut out) beside a sentence. Loading is `SpinnerIcon` with `animate-spin`. Every icon renders a `<div>`, so an icon never sits inside a `<p>` (check:guards `icon-in-paragraph`). Where no Heroicon means the thing, leave the icon out.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity over cleverness: Swedish labels, obvious hierarchy.
2. Earned minimalism: remove what doesn't serve the task, keep compliance context.
3. Numbers are first-class: tabular-nums, alignment, positive/negative clarity.
4. Trust through consistency.
5. Speed is a feature: optimize for the 90-second session.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI). Keyboard-navigable with a visible blue focus ring (`focus-visible:ring-2 ring-ring/70`, 3.2:1 on the panel; fields use the soft halo `field-focus` plus their hairline). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-reduced-transparency`. Color is never the sole state indicator (glyph shape, text). Touch targets ≥40px (`pointer-coarse:min-h-10`, 44px for mobile-critical). Icon-only buttons need `aria-label`.

## Locked UI conventions

They apply system-wide and override anything below that conflicts.

1. **Frame layout.** The page is a rounded panel (12px) on the warm frame, edged by `shadow-panel`. The panel is full-bleed: no centered max-width column, 24px side padding (16px on a phone), and content starts directly under a 48px top bar.
2. **The page title lives in the top bar.** PageHeader renders as the panel's sticky, frosted 48px top bar: 13px/500 title in the strong ink, the `?` help beside it, the primary action on the right, the description hidden. The restyle is the `.page-header` CSS in `app/globals.css`; pages keep using PageHeader (or its `page-header*` class hooks on a hand-rolled header) and never draw a second title. Titles match the menu label ("Verifikationer", not "Bokföring").
3. **Buttons are 6px rectangles with one height per job** (`components/ui/button.tsx`, never per page). Sizes: `default` h-7 (28px: rows, lists, dialogs, forms), `toolbar` h-8 (32px: beside a picker, ToolbarSearch or SegmentedControl and everything in the top bar, check:guards `toolbar-button-size`), `lg` h-11 (auth and touch-critical actions), `icon` h-7, `icon-toolbar` h-8. Looks: `default` is the primary (translucent ink, one per page), `outline` the raised button, `ghost` the quiet one (Avbryt), `row` / `row-primary` the quiet row actions, `destructive` a red tint for irreversible actions, `link`, `success` the green confirmation chip. Touch screens get a 40px minimum and `DialogFooter` stacks full-width 44px buttons on phones, so no call site sets `h-*`, `min-h-*` or `py-*` on a `<Button>`; no `rounded-full` on a Button (check:guards `pill-button`). A running action passes `loading={busy}`; never render a `SpinnerIcon` inside a `<Button>`. A split button (`SplitButton`) is a primary pair with a hairline seam; its caret menu remembers the last-used mode.
4. **Table rows are one line.** Secondary info belongs in the detail view or a popover, never as sub-rows in lists. List rows are 44px, ledger rows 36px.
5. **Chips mark exceptions.** Normal states render as muted text; a Badge (22px, 6px radius, a tint of its tone) only when the row deviates. Same chip on every row means the chip is wrong. The green confirmation chip (`DoneChip`) is allowed only while it confirms, before the row folds away.
6. **Attention is one sentence**, not a banner: `AttnLine`, the amber `StatusGlyph` then a 13px line in the body ink with an optional embedded action link. Max one per page. A page may show at most one global notice line from `lib/notices` (additional notices collapse behind "+N till") plus at most one page-domain attention line.
7. **Help text lives behind a "?"** right after the title: a 16px raised dot opening a popover anchored at it. No instructional copy in the page flow. One exception, Kopplingar only: a connection row shows the first sentence of its help as one muted line under the connection's name (see convention 15).
8. **The fiscal year is app-wide context**, in the company row that heads the sidebar ("2026"); pages read it (`useFiscalScope`) and carry no year picker of their own. A page keeps an in-page year choice only where the year is an assertion or a filing choice (the underlag import, helårsmoms). Any other context picker sits far right in the toolbar as a raised button with a chevron.
9. **One primary per page**, in the top bar. Multiple create paths collapse into a split button whose caret menu remembers the last-used mode (persisted in `user_preferences`).
10. **Confirm up front, don't comment afterwards.** Actions that post or send open a small dialog whose footer states the outcome (`DialogConsequence`: "Skapar verifikat A-217", "Kan inte ångras") instead of writing outcome text into the page afterwards.
11. **Content lands with stagger** (`.stagger-enter`), on server render and on client-fetch completion alike.
12. **Status colors are data, not chrome**: green, amber and red only in numbers, glyphs, exception chips, the confirmation chip and destructive actions.
13. **Overlays**: a dialog is top-anchored at 72px (it grows downward), 440px (`size="confirm"`), 560px (`form`, default) or 720px (`wide`); `document` only for a document page beside its controls. Header: a sentence title (15px) and an optional context line; body: sections 20px apart, no boxes inside; footer over a hairline: the consequence on the left, Avbryt (ghost) and one primary on the right. The veil (`bg-veil`) dims the whole window, sidebar included. A right drawer (`SlideOver`) previews an object from a list; invoice and voucher details stay full pages. Pickers (category, account, customer) are anchored popovers, never a dialog. Esc and a click outside close every overlay.
14. **Manual base, AI as opt-in.** Base flows work without AI; AI entry points are clearly labeled discrete choices and no AI suggestion posts without Granskning.
15. **Settings speak "Fönster"**: flat hairline rows, never boxed cards (`SettingsGroup` / `SettingsRow` / `SettingsSectionHeader`), switches for toggles, a sticky save bar only when dirty, the sections as the Inställningar area's sidebar, one fixed control column (`--settings-control-w`), container-query rows, the section intro behind the `?`. Row help stays behind the row's `?`, with one named exception: Kopplingar is a catalogue people choose from, so each connection row shows the first sentence of its help as one muted 12px line under the name (`ConnectionLabel` in `ConnectionsSettingsContent.tsx`, split by `lib/settings/first-sentence.ts`), and the `?` remains only on rows whose help has more text, opening the rest. No other settings row carries inline help. Six groups (Företaget, Bokföring, Försäljning, Kopplingar, Du, Byrå) in `lib/navigation/settings-sections.ts`.
16. **One radius per role (the radius ladder).** `rounded-sm` 4px leaves (checkboxes, kbd, skeleton lines), `rounded-md` 6px buttons, chips, tags, segments and menu rows, `rounded-lg` 8px fields, the segmented track and single objects, `rounded-pop` 10px menus, popovers, toasts and floating bars, `rounded-xl` 12px the panel, dialogs and drawers, `rounded-full` only for dots, avatars, switch tracks and progress bars. Bare `rounded`, `rounded-2xl`+ and arbitrary `rounded-[Npx]` are dead vocabulary (check:guards `off-ladder-radius`). Nesting is concentric: a 6px segment inside the 8px track with a 2px inset.
17. **States sit where the first row would be** (canvas Tillstand-v3), centered in that space (founder call 2026-11-02): icon, sentence and actions on one axis. The shell, title bar and toolbar stay. Loading: skeleton rows in the real columns at the real heights inside `LateReveal` (they appear only after 300ms; still under reduced motion). First time: `EmptyState` with one sentence and at most one primary (the toolbar hides while there is nothing to filter); the six pages with a painting (Bokföring, Kundfakturor, Löner, Skattekonto, Transaktioner, Underlag) use `StartCard` instead, the same one sentence and one primary on its self-contained dark surface. No hits: one line in the list with "Rensa sökningen". All done: `EmptyState tone="done"`, the green check and what happens next. A page or list that could not load: `LoadError` in the page with "Försök igen", never a toast. A 4xx answer about the company or year (not applicable, not allowed) is not a failed load: show the server's sentence in `EmptyState`, with no retry. A page a company's legal form does not have says so in the page, never an empty body. A missing record or page: `NotFoundState` (the `not-found.tsx` boundaries). Toasts are for undoable successes (the action is the quiet "Ångra"); a failed action says so where the action is (the dialog footer, the row).
18. **Counts sit where waiting work is the page's point**: in the sidebar only on Behöver dig (the things Hem counts, the number in "Visa alla N som lista"), Rutin (the agent's proposals) and Inkommande (the inbox), each a quiet number, not a bubble; on a page in the segment or chip they belong to (`CountTile`).

## Design System Tokens

**Spacing scale.** Tailwind values `1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12`, plus `0.5`, `1.5`, `2.5` inside controls (the 2px track inset, the 6px icon gap, the 10px button and field padding). No hardcoded pixels in page logic.

| Token | Tailwind | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | `1` | icon padding |
| 8 | `2` | tight inline gaps, toolbar gaps, the list bleed and list cell inset |
| 12 | `3` | row padding, footer padding |
| 16 | `4` | form gaps, the content top gap |
| 20 | `5` | dialog and drawer insets, sections inside a dialog |
| 24 | `6` | the page gutter |
| 32 | `8` | between page sections (`space-y-8` on the page root) |

**Radius ladder**: see convention 16.

**Layout (frame layout).**
- The dashboard wrapper is `bg-frame`, exactly the window high (`SHELL_FRAME_CLASS` in `app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx`). The page panel (`bg-background rounded-xl shadow-panel`, 10px from the frame's edges, `SHELL_PANEL_CLASS` in `components/dashboard/DashboardNav.tsx`) holds the area sidebar and `<main>`; `<main>` is its right column with its own inner scroll (`MAIN_PANEL_CLASS` in the layout). Never restyle per page.
- The panel is the desktop scroll container: `position: sticky` binds to it; never assume `window` scroll on dashboard pages. Scroll reset on navigation lives in `MainContainer`.
- Navigation (the V7 shell, to the prototype, founder call 2026-11-15; the rail names its areas, founder call 2026-11-17): the rail is the one part on the frame (`--rail-w` 96px: each area is a 44px button with a 10px radius, a 20px icon and the area's name under it in 12px/500, always visible and never only in a tooltip, 4px apart, 12px between groups; the rail is as wide as the longest area name needs in Swedish and English and no wider, and there is no expand toggle; on top, sharing one row, two 40px icon buttons: a plus that lists every area's create action and a magnifier that opens the command palette; the active area on the selected wash; Agenter and Inställningar pinned at the foot). The area sidebar is the LEFT COLUMN OF THE PAGE PANEL (`--side-w`, 220px, 320px from a 1440px window up), divided from the page by a hairline: the company row (initials tile, name, fiscal year, up-down chevron; it is the company switcher), the area's name (15px/600) with the command K hint, its create button (outline, 28px, a plus or the upload arrow), its rows (30px, 6px radius, 13px in the strong ink, a 16px icon, the active row on the selected wash, `components/dashboard/nav-row-class.ts`), ONE Inställningar row for the area's settings pages (a gear, the first page's name, a chevron; a menu when there are several), and the compact user menu at the foot. No brand mark in the shell on Accounted's own host; a byrå's logo heads the sidebar. `--nav-w` is rail plus sidebar, where the page starts: a `calc()` of the two in `app/globals.css`, never a number of its own, so a width is written once. Banners (sandbox, system notice) sit above the rail and the panel across the full width and take their height from the panel. The tree is `components/dashboard/nav-v3.ts`; the area and row a URL lights are derived there (`resolveNavPlace`), never per page.
- Main container: `PADDED_CANVAS_CLASS` (`px-4 pb-8 pt-4 md:px-6`), no max-width (`components/dashboard/MainContainer.tsx`); `/e/*` and `/chat` render full-bleed, and an extension page that is a plain page (`ExtensionWorkspaceShell`) applies the same class itself. The top padding is what the sticky top bar pulls back over, so it moves in step with the `.page-header` CSS. While the floating assistant button is on screen the bottom padding grows so the last row of a page scrolls clear of it (`AGENT_FAB_CLEARANCE_CLASS` in `components/ui/fab-clearance.ts`); a bottom bar with an action at its right end spreads `PAGE_BOTTOM_BAR_PROPS` from the same file and the button lifts above it.
- Page root: `<div className="space-y-8">`; a list page's toolbar sits 16px under the top bar.
- Narrow widths: from 1024px down the layout is the same frame with fewer columns (drop or shorten a column before reaching for a breakpoint); below `md` the panel dissolves into full-width document flow with the bottom nav (`--bottom-nav-h`), and every fixed or sticky bottom surface offsets by it (`bottom-[calc(var(--bottom-nav-h)+1.5rem)]` for a floating bar). Rows go two-column on their own width (container queries), never on a viewport breakpoint.

**Tabular display rules.**
- All financial values get `tabular-nums`; dates in lists `.date-figures`.
- Right-align numeric columns (`text-right`), and put sums under the amount column.
- Group bands inside tables: a 12px/500 muted label row over a hairline, no fill.

**Date formatting.** Two helpers in `lib/utils.ts`: `formatDate(x)` → `2026-05-11` (accounting data: transaction, invoice, payment and voucher dates) and `formatDateLong(x)` → `11 maj 2026` (metadata: created, linked, verified, expires). Never render a raw date.

**Currency.** `formatCurrency(n, currency?)` from `lib/utils.ts`. Default SEK. Amounts use the U+2212 minus.

**Forbidden / dead patterns.**
- Cards or bordered boxes inside the panel (convention "no cards inside the panel"). A Card is a flat section; only `variant="object"` draws a surface.
- KPI stat strips (a row of number cards under the toolbar) on list pages: counts go into the segments and chips, sums under the amount column.
- Page descriptions that paraphrase the title.
- Two status indicators on the same element.
- Uppercase or tracked labels (check:guards `small-caps-label`), Hedvig on anything but a headline number, `font-medium` on Hedvig.
- Pills: `rounded-full` on buttons (check:guards `pill-button`), chips, pickers or segments.
- Hand-rolled icon buttons: use `Button size="icon"` (28px) or `"icon-toolbar"` (32px).
- Color-coded status from the Tailwind palette (`bg-amber-100`, `bg-emerald-500/10`): use Badge, StatusGlyph and the tokens.
- Tailwind's `shadow-sm` / `shadow-md` / `shadow-lg` anywhere: use the named shadows.
- `active:scale-[...]` outside the Button and chip primitives: the press motion is theirs.
- `bg-gradient-to-*` on page or card backgrounds.
- Opacity-suffixed border classes (`border-border/30`): dividers are `border-b-hairline`, outlines full `border-border`.
- Hover tints other than `hover:bg-secondary/35` (rows) and `hover:bg-secondary/60` (everything else).
- Arbitrary text sizes off the scale: `text-[11px]` only in count and key tiles, `text-[12px]`, `text-[13px]`, `text-[15px]`, `text-[20px]` and the named sizes; no 9, 10, 10.5, 11.5, 12.5, 13.5, 14 or 17px.
- `focus:ring-*`: focus rings are keyboard-only, `focus-visible:ring-*`.
- `transition-all`, durations other than 100/150/300, `ease-[cubic-bezier(...)]` literals.
- `window.confirm` / `alert()`: use `useDestructiveConfirm()`.
- A toast for a page that could not load, or for an outcome that cannot be undone.

**Enforcement.** The rules about buttons, motion, shadows, borders, hover tints, raw colours, native dialogs, focus rings, text sizes, small-caps labels and decorative animation are checked by `check:guards` (`scripts/checks/ui-uniformity.mjs`, hard fail, no baseline); the radius ladder by `off-ladder-radius` in `scripts/checks/no-new-antipatterns.mjs`. A new rule here should come with a guard.
